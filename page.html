TRUMP FIRES NEW TARIFF SALVO AT 14 NATIONS

China faces 20% on top of existing duties; EU and Japan capped at 15%. Markets reel as Nasdaq sinks nearly 4%.

The new duties target subsidised factories flooding world markets, Washington says. — Reuters
